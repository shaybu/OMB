    Get started in 10 minutes

    Before you start, check that Claude Code, OpenCode or Codex already works for you in the terminal.

    Install the app

    Download the OMB desktop app from our internal page and open it.

    Connect an engine

    OMB finds the engines on your machine. Pick Claude Code, OpenCode or Codex.

    Create a bot

    Click + → New bot, then Set me up. Give it a name, a job and a working folder.

    Add your tools

    Plugins → MCP servers → Add server. Add only what this bot needs: Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket or Zephyr.

    Send a small task

    Ask for one clear thing. Keep approvals on Ask for approval and watch how it works.

    Before each workdayRun the daily login tool first. Without a valid token (about 8 hours), the engines get a 403 error and bots can't answer.

    Features

    Everything you can use in our setup, in one place. Filter by area or search.

    You stay in control

    Before a bot runs a command, changes a file or writes to a system, it shows an approval card. You choose how often each bot asks.

    A Chief of Staff's level also applies to the work it hands to other bots.

    From one bot to a whole team

    Put bots in a room, give the team a Chief of Staff, and send one request. The Chief plans, hands out work, and brings back one answer.

    Room

    A group chat for bots, with shared instructions and a shared folder. Use @name to ask one bot.

    Chief of Staff

    Delegates, follows up and summarises. It can also create new specialist bots, with Undo.

    Departments

    Group bots by team in the sidebar. Each department can have its own Chief.
